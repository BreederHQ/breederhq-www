# BreederHQ Pricing

Last updated: 2026-08-24
Canonical page: https://breederhq.com/pricing
Currency: USD

BreederHQ is breeding-program management software for dogs, cats, horses, goats, sheep, cattle, alpacas, llamas, and rabbits.

## Access Is Currently Invitation Only

BreederHQ is not open for self-serve signup. Access is granted to a limited number of founding members, onboarded one at a time.

There is no public price list and no way to sign yourself up. This is deliberate: every founding member receives a founder-led setup, and onboarding capacity is the constraint that limits how many breeders can start at once.

**How to get access:** join the waitlist at https://breederhq.com/pricing. Applications are reviewed in the order they arrive, and we reach out directly when a spot opens.

## What A Founding Member Receives

BreederHQ Launch is a complete setup, not just a software subscription. Founding members receive a founder-led setup that includes moving their existing records onto the platform and live onboarding sessions, so the platform is running properly before they are working in it alone.

The full scope and pricing of BreederHQ Launch are discussed directly with each applicant.

## Large-Scale Operations And Breed Registries

Operations running multiple locations with staff, and breed associations or registries evaluating platform modernization, are handled as a direct conversation rather than a published price.

This track covers team management with role-based access, multi-location management, organization-wide audit trails, API access for third-party integrations, bulk operations, and studbook or member-registry implementation.

Contact: https://breederhq.com/contact

## Marketplace Listing Fees

Listing fees are separate from platform access, and are **currently free during early access**. Standard per-listing fees will begin later. They are always flat, never a percentage of a sale.

BreederHQ never takes a cut of animal transactions. Breeders handle their own transactions and keep every dollar of their deals. Buyers pay nothing to browse, message, join a waitlist, or transact.

This is a deliberate difference from extractive-broker marketplaces such as GoodDog or PuppyFind, which take a percentage of breeder revenue and in some cases charge buyers as well. BreederHQ revenue comes from breeder access and breed-club partnerships, not from a toll on every animal sale.

## Frequently Asked

- **How much does BreederHQ cost?** There is no public price list. Access is invitation only, and pricing is discussed directly with each founding member. Join the waitlist at https://breederhq.com/pricing.
- **Can I sign up right now?** No. There is no self-serve signup. Join the waitlist and we will reach out when a spot opens.
- **Is there a free trial?** No. The 14-day free trial has been retired. Founding members receive a founder-led setup instead.
- **Is there a free tier?** No.
- **Why is there no price on the pricing page?** Spots are limited by onboarding capacity, not by software licences. Every founding member gets a personal setup, so BreederHQ onboards breeders one at a time rather than selling seats at scale.
- **What species does BreederHQ support?** Nine: Dogs, Cats, Horses, Goats, Sheep, Cattle, Alpacas, Llamas, Rabbits.
- **Does the marketplace charge transaction fees?** No. BreederHQ never takes a percentage of a sale.
- **Is BreederHQ audit-ready?** Yes. Health records, vaccination logs, medication logs with withdrawal periods, milk and production records, parasite-management logs, ownership provenance, timestamped signed contracts, financial ledger, and movement records are exportable and timestamped for USDA/APHIS, DHIA/DHIR, ADGA Linear Appraisal, FAMACHA/SCSRPC, USDA Scrapie, Brucellosis/bTB/BQA, National Dairy FARM, Coggins/EIA, USEF, ARBA, CFA/TICA, and breed-club registration audits, plus IRS, accountant, and insurance audits.
- **Does BreederHQ integrate with genetics labs, chip companies, or governing bodies?** Integration partnerships are an active motion. Target partners include Embark, Wisdom Panel, GenSol Diagnostics, Paw Print Genetics, UC Davis VGL, and other labs; AKC Reunite, HomeAgain, AVID, Datamars, and other chip companies; AKC, UKC, TKC, AQHA, Jockey Club, Holstein Association, ADGA, ARBA, CFA, TICA, OFA, CHIC, and other governing bodies. Scout AI's Genetics Scanner and receipt-capture reduce manual data entry today while direct partner integrations continue to ship.
